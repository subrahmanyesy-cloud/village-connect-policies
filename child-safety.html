Child Safety Standards Policy

Village Connect is committed to maintaining a safe and trusted environment for all users. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, or any activity that endangers children.

Our Commitment

Village Connect strictly prohibits:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)
  • Child sexual exploitation or abuse
  • Grooming or predatory behavior
  • Sexualization of minors
  • Human trafficking involving minors
  • Any content or activity that threatens child safety
Any account found engaging in such activities will be permanently suspended and may be reported to appropriate law enforcement agencies.

Reporting Safety Concerns

Users can report content, users, or activities that may violate this policy through:

  • Support / Help section within the application
  • Email support channel listed below

All reports are reviewed promptly and appropriate action is taken.

Content Moderation

Village Connect reserves the right to:

  • Review reported content and user activity
  • Remove violating content immediately
  • Suspend or terminate user accounts
  • Cooperate with law enforcement agencies when legally required

Compliance with Applicable Laws

Village Connect complies with applicable child protection laws and regulations. Confirmed child sexual abuse material and related violations may be reported to the relevant authorities in accordance with applicable legal requirements.
