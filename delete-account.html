Village Connect - Permanent Account Deletion Request

Users can request deletion of their Village Connect account and associated personal data by contacting us.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to: villageconnect487@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject: Account Deletion Request
  3. Include your registered mobile number.

Data Deleted

  • User profile information
  • Contact information
  • Service request history
  • Uploaded audio recordings

Data Retained

  • Transaction and payment records may be retained where required by law.
  • Security and fraud prevention records may be retained for a limited period.

Retention Period

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 15 days.

For assistance, contact: villageconnect487@gmail.com
