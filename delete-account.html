Village Connect allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to the address below.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your registered mobile number.
  4. Our team will verify the request and process the account deletion.
📧 villageconnect487@gmail.com

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • User profile information
  • Registered mobile number
  • Service request history
  • Uploaded audio recordings
  • Other personal information associated with the account

Data That May Be Retained

  • Transaction and payment records where required by law
  • Security, fraud prevention, and dispute resolution records
  • Information required to comply with legal obligations
Account deletion requests are generally processed within 30 days of verification. Certain records may be retained for a longer period where required by applicable law or for legitimate business and security purposes.
