Village Connect Privacy Policy

Village Connect ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Village Connect, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Who We Are

Village Connect is a hyperlocal community platform that helps neighbours within the same village or town connect for small offline assistance such as collecting medicines, groceries, parcels, or documents.

Village Connect acts only as a communication platform. We do not perform, supervise, guarantee, or participate in services exchanged between users.

2. Information We Collect

  • Mobile number used for OTP login.
  • Full name.
  • Town and preferred locations.
  • Help categories selected by you.
  • Requests posted through the platform.
  • Ratings, reports, notifications, and platform activity.

We do not collect or store your bank account details, debit card details, credit card details, or UPI credentials.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your identity using OTP.
  • To show relevant local requests.
  • To notify users about nearby opportunities.
  • To connect requestors and helpers.
  • To improve trust through ratings and reports.
  • To maintain and improve platform security.

4. Information Visible To Other Users

  • Your name.
  • Your town.
  • Your rating.
  • Your selected help categories.

Your phone number is shared only after a request is mutually accepted between both parties.

5. Notifications

Village Connect may send notifications regarding:

  • Nearby help requests.
  • Status updates.
  • Activity related to your requests.
  • Platform announcements.

You can disable new help request notifications from your profile settings at any time.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Your information may only be shared:

  • With another user after request acceptance.
  • With service providers required to operate the platform.
  • When legally required by government authorities.

7. Data Retention and Security

We retain information while your account remains active and as required for security, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

Although reasonable security measures are used, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices

  • Update your profile information anytime.
  • Update your help categories anytime.
  • Manage notification preferences.
  • Request account deletion through the app.

9. Children

Village Connect is intended for adults and is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.

10. Safety and Responsibility

Village Connect only enables users to connect with each other. Users are solely responsible for their interactions, arrangements, and transactions conducted outside the platform.

Always verify identities and exercise caution when meeting people offline.

11. Changes To This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of Village Connect after updates indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
