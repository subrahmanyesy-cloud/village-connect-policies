Refund Policy

Village Connect - Connecting Neighbours 🤝
Village Connect charges credits only to workers for accessing and responding to service opportunities available on the platform.

Credit Purchases

  • Credits purchased by workers are generally non-refundable.
  • If credits are deducted due to a technical issue, duplicate deduction, or system error, the worker may contact Village Connect for review.
  • After verification, eligible credits may be restored to the worker's account.
  • No refund will be provided for credits that have already been used to access opportunities or services.
  • Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of the original transaction.
Village Connect reserves the right to review each refund request individually and determine eligibility based on platform records and transaction history.
